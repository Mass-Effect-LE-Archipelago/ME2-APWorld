# Mass Effect Legendary Edition 2

## What does randomization do to this game?

Recruitment dossiers, the Normandy's three suicide-mission upgrades (Heavy Ship Armor, Multicore Shielding, Thanix
Cannon), Reaper IFF Intel, weapon unlocks and resource bundles are shuffled into the multiworld. You play the opening as
normal and take command of the Normandy SR-2; from there, a recruitment mission opens when its dossier arrives, and the
Derelict Reaper opens once you hold enough Reaper IFF Intel.

## What counts as a check?

Finishing the prologue and Freedom's Progress, each recruitment, Horizon, the Collector Ship, the Reaper IFF, each
loyalty mission, N7 assignments (default), hub assignments (optional) and DLC missions. Collector Base checks are past
the point of no return and only hold filler.

## What is the goal?

Destroy or keep the Collector Base and finish the game with Shepard alive. With the default readiness setting, logic
expects every loyalty mission and all three ship upgrades before the Omega 4 Relay, so a successful run is always
possible. Choosing the right specialists is still up to you.

## Status

Early development. The world generates and the client and bridge exist, but the in-game mod that hides missions until
their dossier arrives is not built yet. See `docs/SCOPE.md` in the repository.
